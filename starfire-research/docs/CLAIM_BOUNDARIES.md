# Claim Boundaries

This document defines the strongest claims currently supported by the Starfire experimental program and the claims that remain unsupported.

## Supported architectural claim

The H9-H11 sequence supports the following narrow statement:

> Under frozen symbolic intervention regimes, Starfire can construct or discover a finite candidate relation frontier, infer an evidence-supported rule, independently recompute the discovery and proof, admit only a validated certificate into typed executable state, and use that state as a causally necessary intermediate for later bounded reasoning across unseen vocabularies.

The support for this claim depends on all of the following:

- deterministic symbolic task construction;
- finite candidate search;
- fixed evidence and scoring contracts;
- independent proof recomputation;
- typed executable admission;
- provenance-bearing state transitions;
- matched compute budgets;
- causal controls;
- exact replay;
- independent objective checking;
- held-out and future vocabulary transfer.

## H9-specific claim

H9 supports:

> A validated same-root operation can create an executable commitment that is causally necessary for a later fixed-budget operation within the PECS shadow substrate.

H9 does not establish learned rule induction because the target bridge was directly witnessed.

## H10-specific claim

H10 supports:

> A deterministic, target-blind proposer can infer a useful symbolic rule from a fixed intervention table, survive independent proof recomputation, and create a causally necessary executable commitment.

H10 does not establish autonomous candidate-vocabulary invention because the candidate antecedent and consequent sets were supplied by the experiment.

## H11-specific claim

H11 supports:

> A candidate relation frontier can be discovered from raw symbolic graph incidence without explicitly supplying the target relation endpoints, after which the useful rule can be inferred, independently validated, and admitted as causally necessary executable state.

H11 does not establish learned frontier criteria or learned representations.

## Unsupported claims

The current evidence does not support claims of:

- artificial general intelligence;
- consciousness or phenomenal experience;
- sentience, personhood, or subjective continuity;
- human-level cognition;
- open-world causal discovery;
- unrestricted autonomous reasoning;
- natural-language evidence extraction;
- learned ontology formation;
- unbounded relation invention;
- learned scoring laws;
- learned validation thresholds;
- reliable self-modification;
- safe autonomous deployment;
- live-routing readiness;
- general transfer outside the frozen symbolic regimes.

## Terminology restrictions

Public descriptions should avoid presenting internal proxies as established cognitive properties.

Use:

- `reservoir statistic` rather than `consciousness measurement`;
- `persistent state` rather than `genuine personal continuity`;
- `autonomous background operation` rather than `independent existence`;
- `symbolic transfer across held-out vocabularies` rather than `general intelligence`;
- `proof-carrying executable state` rather than `understanding`, unless understanding is explicitly defined operationally.

## Inference versus evidence

Every public claim should be identified as one of:

1. **Direct experimental result** — measured under a frozen contract.
2. **Architectural interpretation** — an inference from one or more results.
3. **Research hypothesis** — a proposed explanation not yet established.
4. **Speculation** — a possibility offered without evidentiary support.

Architectural interpretations must not be presented as direct measurements.

## Negative-result policy

A failed or invalid experiment remains part of the public record.

- `REJECTED` means the frozen scientific gates were not met.
- `CONTROL_FAILURE` means the experiment cannot identify the intended causal effect.
- `REPLAY_FAILURE` means deterministic comparison is not valid.
- `INFRASTRUCTURE_FAILURE` means the execution contract itself failed.

A later mechanism may supersede an earlier failure, but it does not retroactively convert that earlier experiment into a pass.

## Current confidence boundary

The strongest reasonable interpretation is architectural rather than general-cognitive:

> Starfire has demonstrated a controlled mechanism for converting validated symbolic evidence into executable intermediate state whose admission changes later bounded capability.

The experiments do not yet show that Starfire can discover the representations, abstractions, objectives, or validation laws required for open-ended cognition.
